Your first steps

Start your
adventure.

A whole new hobby can feel like a lot. It doesn’t have to be. Try one small step, ask questions, and find the part you enjoy most.

The short version

Four steps.
Zero pressure.

You don’t need a painted army or a rulebook memorized to start. The club can help you explore each part at your own pace.

01 / EXPLORE

See a game

Watch a match, ask what’s happening, and get a feel for the battlefield before you make any decisions.

02 / PICK

Find your force

Choose a faction or setting that catches your eye. Start with the look, story, or playstyle you find exciting.

03 / CREATE

Build & paint

Try assembling and painting a miniature. You can learn the basics from club members before investing in supplies.

04 / PLAY

Roll the dice

Learn the rules in a small, friendly game. Nobody expects you to know everything on your first turn.

Good questions to ask

Before your
first battle.

Wondering where to begin? These are the essentials. For current club details, just reach out.

Do I need to bring miniatures?

No need to buy anything before you ask. Check with the club about what models or demo games are available for your first visit.

Do I need to know the rules?

Not at all. The rules make more sense once you see them in action, and a club member can walk you through a first game.

What should I try first?

Start with whatever interests you: watching a game, learning about a setting, painting, or trying a small match. There’s no one correct entry point.

Questions are welcome
