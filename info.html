What we’re about

The hobby is
better together.

The SHHS Warhammer Club brings tabletop battles and miniature hobbying together. Whether you’ve got a whole collection or you’re only curious, there’s a way to take part.

01 / PLAY

Tabletop battles

Learn the game, test a strategy, and see how the story changes with every roll.

02 / MAKE

Miniature hobby

Build and paint tiny characters, personalize a force, and share what you’re working on.

03 / CONNECT

Good company

Meet fellow fans, trade ideas, learn together, and welcome new players to the table.

Your first visit

Curious is
all you need.

You don’t need an army, a painting kit, or any experience to say hello. Get in touch first and we’ll help you figure out what to expect and what to bring.

When and where does the club meet?

Ask us for the current meeting schedule and location. We’ll share the latest details directly.

Can I come if I’m brand new?

Absolutely. Tell us you’re new and we can suggest a simple first step, whether that’s watching a game, painting, or learning the basics.

What if I already have an army?

Bring it along when you’ve checked the current meetup details. We’d love to hear what you collect and what you like to play.
